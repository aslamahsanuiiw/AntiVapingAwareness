What's Inside A Vape?

Vaping smoke may look harmless, but it contains dangerous chemicals. Many are found in things like antifreeze, nail polish remover, and paint thinner. Teens often don’t know what they inhale, including carcinogens that cause cancer.

Vapes aren’t just simple machines. Here’s what’s inside and why each chemical is harmful to the body.

Nicotine

Addictive substance affecting teen brains, mood, behavior, and long-term addiction.

Propylene Glycol

Produces vapor but can damage lungs and throat.

Vegetable Glycerin

Used for thick clouds; safe in food but inhalation causes lung inflammation.

Water

Carries harmful chemicals deep into the lungs.

Heavy Metals

Toxic metals causing brain damage, cancer, and lung disease.

Cadmium

Metal in batteries, damages lungs/kidneys, carcinogenic.

Arsenic

Found in rat poison/pesticides, harms heart, lungs, skin with long-term exposure.

Benzene

Carcinogen in exhaust and cigarette smoke causing blood disorders.

Glycidol

Forms when e-liquids are heated; likely human carcinogen.

Tobacco-Specific Nitrosamines

Cancer-causing chemicals also found in cigarettes.

Volatile Organic Compounds

Toxic gases like formaldehyde damaging lungs and increasing cancer risk.

Diacetyl

Causes “popcorn lung,” leading to coughing, wheezing, permanent damage.

Acrolein

Damages lung tissue and airways.

More Shocking Facts

Scientists don’t fully understand long-term effects yet. Nicotine-free vapes may still contain nicotine. Heating vape liquids creates carcinogens. Heavy metals like lead and nickel reach the lungs. Acrolein damages blood vessels and the heart. Repeated exposure may affect teen brain development.
