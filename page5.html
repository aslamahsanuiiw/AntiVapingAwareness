Step by Step: How Teens Fall Into the Vaping Trap

Step 1: Curiosity

I see it everywhere online. Everyone’s doing it — can it really be that bad?

Step 2: Social Pressure

Friends offer it; don’t want to seem lame or scared.

Step 3: First Try

It was just one puff at a party. No big deal… right?

Step 4: Regular Use

Keep one in my bag, often without noticing.

Step 5: Dependence

Anxious without it, hard to focus, addicted.
