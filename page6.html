Laws and Regulation

Vaping might seem personal, but laws protect teens. Federal and state regulations control sales, marketing, and access to vaping products.

Under 21? Illegal to buy. Schools enforce vape-free zones, and penalties exist for breaking rules. Flavors, marketing, and online sales have strict controls.

Federal Vaping Laws in the United States

Key laws include Tobacco 21 (no sale under 21), ID verification, ad restrictions, and flavored vape bans unless approved.

States like CA, MA, NY, NJ, and RI have banned flavored e-cigarettes. The FDA regulates marketing through the PMTA approval process to ensure public safety.

Federal Key Points

  • Age to buy: 21+
  • Online sales require ID verification
  • All schools are vape-free zones
  • Ad restrictions to protect youth
