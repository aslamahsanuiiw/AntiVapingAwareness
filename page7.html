Conclusion

Vaping isn’t just a harmless trend — it can cause serious diseases, lung damage, and even death. Many teens fall into this trap unaware of the long-term risks and how quickly addiction develops.

This website’s goal is to spread awareness and inspire smarter choices. If you’ve learned something new or reconsidered vaping, it has done its job. Every small action matters — whether helping a friend quit, saying no, or simply sharing what you know.
